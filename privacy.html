The Velvet Plate ("we," "us") provides holiday and event catering in Harvest, Huntsville, Madison and the surrounding North Alabama area. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or request a quote, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us when you fill out a quote or package request form:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Event details such as dates needed, guest count, city, package and dietary preferences
  • Any notes you add, such as allergies or favorite dishes
  • Whether you agreed to receive text messages, and when

When you submit a form we also record the date and time and the page you sent it from. Our website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

2. How we use it

  • To call, email or text you about your catering request
  • To prepare quotes, confirm orders, dates and pickup or delivery details
  • To plan menus around allergies and dietary needs
  • To keep basic business records

We do not sell your personal information.

3. SMS text messaging

If you check the SMS box on our request forms, you agree to receive text messages from The Velvet Plate at the number you provide. These messages are about your catering request, such as confirming we received it, quote follow-ups, order and date confirmations, and pickup or delivery reminders.

  • Optional: Agreeing to texts is not required to request a quote or place an order.
  • Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your request.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You'll get one final message confirming you've been unsubscribed.
  • Help: Reply HELP for help, or call us at (256) 555-0123.
  • Carriers: Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We do not share your information.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

The services we use to send and manage messages only use your number to deliver our messages to you.

Full SMS program details are in the SMS Terms section of our Terms & Conditions.

4. Who we share it with

We share your information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed to handle your request. These include:

  • Our form automation service (Zapier), which passes your request to our team
  • Our email, text messaging and record-keeping tools

These providers may only use your information to provide services to us. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone for their own marketing, and the SMS consent described above is never shared with third parties.

We may also share information if required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

5. How long we keep it

We keep request and order details as long as we need them to serve you and keep normal business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

6. Your choices

  • Stop text messages at any time by replying STOP
  • Ask us to update or correct your information
  • Ask us to delete your information, unless we need to keep it for an open order or a legal reason

To make any of these requests, call us or stop by using the contact details below.

7. Children

Our website and services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
